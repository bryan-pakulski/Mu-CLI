Context growth

total_est vs actual prompt tokens, per iteration
Context growth

Shows: top — the harness's cl100k_base estimate (dashed) vs the provider's reported prompt tokens (solid) per iteration, with compaction events marked. Bottom — a heat strip of which context layer (L0–L5) dominates each iteration, plus a diverging drift strip.

Why: suspect #1 lives here. If the estimate diverges from actual, the compaction budget (computed in cl100k tokens) fires at the wrong point. The heat strip shows which layer is eating the budget — usually L5 history ballooning.

Read: the solid line is the provider's own count — it can never exceed the context window, so it is the honest "how full is the prompt." For Ollama in a warm loop the solid line is the non-cached prompt delta (near-zero = cache hit), not the full prompt; the dashed cl100k line is the harness's fill view in that case. The drift-corrected real estimate and drift ratio are in the hover tooltip; the drift chart carries the estimate-error diagnosis. Blue verticals = compaction.

Tokenizer drift

(actual − est) / actual · 100 — gated to reliable full-prompt readings
Tokenizer drift

Shows: per-iteration drift between the real prompt size and the harness estimate, centered at 0%. The ±15% band is shaded — inside it the estimate is "good enough".

Why: this single chart decides suspect #1. Persistent drift outside the band means the compaction budget is systematically wrong for this model's tokenizer.

Caveat: drift_pct is only computed when the provider reports a real full-prompt count. Ollama's prompt_tokens_actual is the non-cached prompt delta (near-zero in a warm loop) — those iterations are zeroed and flagged drift_pct_reliable=false (rendered as "drift unknown", not "0% = perfect"). The y-axis is clamped to keep the warn band visible; ▲▼ caps mark values beyond the clamp. Toggle signed-log to read the full range. Absolute drift is visible in Context growth.

Tool-output efficiency

store raw externally, observe compactly, retrieve on demand
Tool-output efficiency

Shows: per iteration, raw tool-output tokens (amber, what the tool would have injected) vs. injected tokens (green, the compact observation actually placed in context). The amber band between them is tokens saved by storing raw externally.

Why: the headline metric for tool-output management. A wide amber gap means large results (build logs, file reads, searches) are being observed rather than bloating context; a thin gap means results were small enough to stay verbatim. The cards total the run: compression ratio, tokens saved, results omitted/stored, and retrieval rate.

Read: hover the chart for per-iter numbers. compression ratio = tokens_saved ÷ raw_tokens. retrieval rate = recall/result_* calls ÷ total tool calls — too low with many omitted results may mean stored output isn't being recalled before re-running. Cache counters (evictions/invalidations/disk hits/locator hits) come from the tool-result cache.
